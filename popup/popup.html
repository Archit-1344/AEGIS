            Anti-Phishing Email Gateway & Intelligence System

            A.E.G.I.S. is a browser extension that checks incoming Gmail/Outlook mail for signs of spoofing, scams, and phishing — entirely on your device. Instead of a binary "block or allow," it gives every email a 0–100 trust score with a plain-English explanation, so you can make an informed call on borderline mail instead of just guessing or losing legitimate messages to an overly aggressive filter.

            How it works

            Layered protection with local message analysis — no email body content is sent to an A.E.G.I.S. server:

            • Membrane 0 — Trusted sender: known contacts skip sender-reputation penalties, while risky message content is still checked.
            • Membrane 1 — Authentication: SPF & DMARC record publication via DNS-over-HTTPS, plus optional Gmail/Outlook provider-reported SPF/DKIM/DMARC results through OAuth.
            • Membrane 2 — Visual similarity: typosquat & brand-impersonation detection on sender domain and links.
            • Membrane 3 — Domain age: RDAP lookup for newly-registered domains.
            • Membrane 4 — Attachments: filename/extension check for executable, script, and macro-capable file types.
            • Content & link scan: keyword and link-reputation checks layered on top.
            • Protected Click: suspicious links pause before navigation and reveal the real destination and reasons.
            • Verified Header Mode: optional provider metadata access for Gmail and Outlook; A.E.G.I.S. stores the result summary, never the original header, body or long-lived OAuth token.

            Gmail/Outlook's own spam classification is used only as one minor signal alongside all of the above — never as the deciding factor.
